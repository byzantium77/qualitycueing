What is Quality Cueing?

A teaching philosophy, not a script.

QualityCueing™ is a precise, intentional approach to verbal instruction that tells a student's body exactly what to do — so they can stay in their practice instead of decoding it. It's the difference between a class that needs to be watched and one that can simply be followed.

  • ✓ Scaffolded approach to verbal instruction
  • ✓ Present tense, clear action verbs
  • ✓ Direct language, not passive
  • ✓ Intentional breath instruction
  • ✓ Guided from the ground up
  • ✓ Arrival and deepening cues in every pose
  • ✓ No "um's," "uh's," or repeated words
  • ✓ English first, Sanskrit second
Why it matters

Most teacher training never touches your voice.

Alignment, sequencing, anatomy — most trainings cover all of it. Almost none teach instructors how to actually speak a class into being. QualityCueing™ fills that gap.
