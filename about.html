The people behind the method

Built by two people who actually teach.

QualityCueing™ didn't come from a textbook. It came from a studio, thousands of classes, and two instructors obsessed with getting the words right.

MR

Megan Rader

Founder, QualityCueing™ & Kula Yoga

Megan is the founder of Kula Yoga in Grand Rapids, Michigan, and the creator of QualityCueing™ — a proprietary method for verbal instruction that she developed over years of teaching in the studio.

Frustrated by how much of standard yoga instruction leans on visual demonstration, Megan rebuilt her cueing from the ground up: present tense, direct language, and cues precise enough that a student never has to open their eyes to know what to do. That method now powers every class on yogaudio, and it's the foundation of the training we're building here.

D

Danae

Studio Director & Certified QualityCueing™ Instructor

Danae has managed Kula Yoga for several years, with a track record of real success running the studio day to day. She's led many of Kula's teacher trainings and worked closely with Megan to help develop QualityCueing™ into the system it is today.

She's certified in QualityCueing™ and brings that hands-on training experience directly into how we teach the method to instructors — turning what Megan built into something other teachers can actually learn and apply.

Why we're doing this

Megan and Danae have spent years proving this method works in a real studio. Now they're teaching it to instructors everywhere.
